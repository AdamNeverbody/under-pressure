    Each dot is a position with a character of its own, shown by its colour, and no two are alike. Each is drawn toward others, but not evenly: one can be drawn toward another that isn’t drawn back. Any two that come too close press apart. Nothing in the rules says what shapes to make. Every shape you see forms by itself, and none of them lasts. Press any row below, or its key, to try it.

    • The drift

      Who is drawn to whom changes slowly: this is the drift. For a while each dot is drawn toward dots like itself, and they gather into clumps. Then each is drawn after dots unlike itself, and they stream. The line along the top shows where it is, from streaming at the left to gathering at the right.

    • Sound

      With sound on, each bubble sounds its share against a soft hum that stands for the whole. A share is a ratio, and so is a musical interval, so 2/1 sounds an octave above the whole, 3/2 a fifth above and 1/2 an octave below. A bubble is in tune while it holds its share and slides out of tune when it is too full or too empty. It wavers as its dots stray from going round together, swells once each time round, splits into three voices when it breaks, and plucks when it pops. With bloops, bells or plucked strings it sounds as it goes round instead of holding its note. Shares below 1/7 and above 18/1 would be too low or too high to hear well, so they are moved by whole octaves until they can be.
